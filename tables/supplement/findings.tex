Across all 1720 pairs (1000 in scheme~A, 720 in scheme~B), no certified lower bound exceeded the reference value and no certified upper bound fell below it. The certificate $U_\beta$ never fell below the Richardson estimate of $L(c_{\mathrm{CO}})$, as \cref{thm:cert} requires. The upper side is tight: the median of $\mathrm{UB}/\rho_\mathcal{N}-1$ is 0.03\% (scheme~A) and 0.03\% (scheme~B), and its 95th percentile is 1.7\% and 1.8\%. The certified relative width is below $1\%$ for 65.7\% and 37.4\% of pairs, and below $5\%$ for 91.0\% and 72.8\%. The point estimate $\hat\rho$ of \cref{alg:cert} has median relative error 0.02\% and 0.03\%. Its 95th percentile is 1.7\% and 1.8\%, but its maximum is 16.3\% and 4.4\%: for strongly separated, ill-conditioned pairs every curve in the menu is far from geodesic, and only the certified interval should be trusted. The width is driven by the lower bound. $\rho_{\mathrm{marg}}$ improves on $\rho_{\mathrm{CO}}$ in 23.4\% of scheme-A pairs. Almost all of these are univariate pairs (199 of 200 at $d=1$, where $\rho_{\mathrm{marg}}$ is exact); the rest are at most $12\%$ of pairs per dimension for $d\ge2$. In scheme~B it improves on $\rho_{\mathrm{CO}}$ in 5.4\% of pairs, concentrated at $d=2$ with $s\ge3$ (45\% of those pairs). Among the curves, $c_{\mathrm{CO}}$ is the best in the large majority of cases (\cref{tab:mcA,tab:mcB}). Among all upper bounds, $U_{\mathrm{SPC}}$ attains the minimum in 47.2\% of scheme-A pairs, including every $d=1$ pair, where it is exact. In scheme~B it attains the minimum in 9.7\% of pairs, mostly for $d=2$ and $s\ge3$. This is consistent with $U_{\mathrm{SPC}}$ being the length of an explicit curve inside the diagonal submodel (\cref{rem:spc}). Including it in the menu of \cref{alg:cert} costs one eigendecomposition.
