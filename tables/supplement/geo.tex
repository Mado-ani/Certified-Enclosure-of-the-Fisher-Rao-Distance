For the 1707 pairs on which single shooting converged, we evaluated the enclosure of \cref{thm:geo} in ball arithmetic, with $T=16$ and $T=32$. The shooting velocity was used as given, and the endpoint mismatch $e$ is at most $4\times10^{-7}$. The enclosure contained the reference value in every case (0 violations). Its relative width has median 0.04\%, 95th percentile 0.29\% and maximum 0.61\% at $T=32$. The median ratio of the widths at $T=16$ and $T=32$ is 4.00, the rate $T^{-2}$ predicted by \cref{thm:geo}. For comparison, the unconditional width obtained by combining the same upper bound with $\max(\rho_{\mathrm{CO}},\rho_{\mathrm{marg}})$ has median 0.85\%. In 1509 of the 1707 pairs, the conditional lower bound improved on the unconditional one. The remaining 198 pairs are all univariate, where $\rho_{\mathrm{marg}}$ is already exact. In no case did the conditional lower bound exceed an unconditional upper bound, which is consistent with the shooting geodesics being minimising (\cref{rem:minimal}).
